About DocuMind

DocuMind is a Text and Speech Analysis project: a document question-answering system built on classic NLP retrieval.

NLP pipeline

Text extraction, cleaning, sentence and word tokenization (NLTK), stop-word removal, stemming, overlapping three-sentence passages, TF-IDF vectors (scikit-learn), cosine-similarity retrieval of the best passages, then sentence ranking inside them. Questions starting with "when" or "how many" get a small boost for sentences containing dates or numbers.

Technology stack

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Python and Flask REST API. SQLite. pypdf and python-docx for file reading. Chart.js for charts.

Limitations

DocuMind extracts existing sentences; it does not reason, summarize across sections or do arithmetic. It matches keywords and word weights, so it can miss paraphrased questions and cannot read scanned PDFs. "Match strength" is a heuristic from similarity scores, not an accuracy percentage. Answers may be incomplete or wrong; verify them against the document for academic or professional use.

Future enhancements

Semantic embeddings, a transformer reader model (for example SQuAD-trained), OCR for scanned files, multi-document search, speech input, and evaluation with F1 and exact match on a QA dataset.
