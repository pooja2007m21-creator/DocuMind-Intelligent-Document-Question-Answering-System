Ask a document. Get answers from the document.

Upload a PDF, Word or text file, ask a question in plain English, and DocuMind returns the most relevant sentences, with their source passages shown so you can verify them.

Open workspaceHow it works

Answers only from your file

Nothing is generated from outside knowledge. If the document does not contain an answer, DocuMind says so.

Evidence you can check

Every answer shows the passages it came from, with the answer sentences highlighted.

History and analytics

Questions and documents are saved in SQLite. Search past answers and track how often questions are answered.

Answers are extracted by an NLP retrieval method and may be incomplete or wrong. Check them against the source before relying on them.
